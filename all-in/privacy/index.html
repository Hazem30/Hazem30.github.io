Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information ALL IN: Confidently Wrong (“ALL IN”, “the game”) collects, why, how long it is kept and the choices you have. ALL IN is operated by Hazem Elghonimy, an independent developer based in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for your personal data in the game.

The short version: you can play without giving us your name or email. We keep what the game needs to run matches fairly and safely, delete player-written text quickly, show ads through Google AdMob, and process purchases through Apple. We do not sell your personal data.

1. What we collect

  • Player ID. When you first play online, the game creates an anonymous account with a random player ID. It is not linked to your name, phone number or email.
  • Linked sign-in (optional). If you choose to link Sign in with Apple or Google so your purchases follow you to other devices, we receive an account identifier from that provider and, if you share it, your email address (Apple lets you hide it behind a relay address). We do not receive your password.
  • Gameplay. Matches you play: your seat, chosen character, bids, answers, scores, the questions you have been dealt (so you are not shown the same ones too often), the room or queue you joined, and timestamps. Seat names are preset (for example “Player 2”); you do not enter a name.
  • Text you write. In private rooms where typed bluffs are switched on, the fake answers you type (up to 60 characters). They are checked automatically for links, contact details and blocked words before other players see them.
  • Safety records. Players you block, reports you file or that are filed about something you wrote, and any temporary typing pause on your account.
  • Purchases. Which in-app purchases your account owns (for example ad removal). Payment is handled by Apple; we never receive your card or bank details.
  • Support requests. If you contact us through the support form: your email address, topic, message, language and, if you provide it, your player ID.
  • Technical data. To stop abuse, we count requests per player ID and, for the support form, per one-way hash of your IP address. We never store your raw IP address for this. Our hosting providers keep standard server logs.
  • Advertising data. Google AdMob shows ads in the game and may collect device identifiers, IP address, approximate location and ad interactions. On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is only used if you allow tracking when asked.
  • On your device only. Your vibration setting and whether you have seen the tutorial are saved on your device and never sent to us.

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not ask for contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the game (performing our agreement with you): running matches, matchmaking, private rooms, scores, rematches and reconnecting you to a match.
  • To keep players safe and the game fair (our legitimate interests and legal obligations): screening typed text, reports, blocks, temporary typing pauses, rate limits and preventing cheating.
  • To deliver what you bought (performing our agreement): recording and restoring your purchases.
  • To show ads (your consent where the law requires it, otherwise our legitimate interests): ads fund the free game. Personalised ads are only shown where you have agreed.
  • To answer you: replying to support and privacy requests.

3. Who we share it with

We use service providers who process data for us under their own terms and security commitments:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland): accounts, game database, server functions and web hosting. Game data is stored in Google’s Belgium (europe-west1) region.
  • Google AdMob: advertising. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Apple: in-app purchase payments and, if you choose it, Sign in with Apple.
  • RevenueCat: verifies purchases and keeps a record of what your anonymous player ID owns.
  • Google (Gmail): delivers support requests to our inbox.

Other players see what the game shows in a match: your preset seat name, character, bids once revealed, scores and the answers you wrote after the reveal. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect players’ safety. We do not sell your personal data for money.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the game: Settings & Account → Delete account. This removes your player ID, memberships, blocks, questions seen, restrictions and sign-in, and anonymises reports you filed.
  • Ads: you can decline tracking when iOS asks, change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and change your ad consent choices from the game’s privacy options where they apply. Buying ad removal stops ads.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (including under Saudi Arabia’s Personal Data Protection Law, the EU and UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict some uses, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of “sale” or “sharing” for targeted advertising. Send requests through the support form, choosing “Privacy or my data”. We reply within the time the law requires and will not treat you differently for using your rights.
  • You may also complain to your data protection authority, such as the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the European Union and the United States. Where required, they rely on recognised safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Answers, bids and other secrets of a match are kept on the server and only revealed when the rules allow. Access to backend data is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly if something goes wrong.

8. Children

ALL IN is for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell you in the game.

10. Contact

Hazem Elghonimy, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Contact us through the support form.

  • احذف حسابك في أي وقت من داخل اللعبة: الإعدادات والحساب ← حذف الحساب. يزيل ذلك معرّف اللاعب، والعضويات، والحظر، والأسئلة التي ظهرت لك، والقيود، وتسجيل الدخول، ويجعل البلاغات التي قدّمتها مجهولة المصدر.

  • يمكنك أيضًا تقديم شكوى إلى الجهة المختصة بحماية البيانات، مثل الهيئة السعودية للبيانات والذكاء الاصطناعي (سدايا).
